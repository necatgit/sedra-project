# 2026-11-22: The week in review: repository public, advisory out, testnet waiting for a go-ahead

Covers 2026-11-16 to 2026-11-22.

## This repository is public, with the operator advisory

- **Security advisory for Sedra node operators** ([research/0007](../research/0007-sedrad-v2.0.0-advisory.md)):
  written and reviewed on 2026-11-18, published on 2026-11-21 together with this repository. sedrad v2.0.0 nodes with
  the RPC or P2P port open to the internet can be frozen or crashed from outside. The advisory gives the impact and
  the protection steps, not how to cause the problems. **There is no fixed sedrad release yet**; whether the pools and
  the exchange were told, and whether the planned maintenance release fixes both issues, is asked (Q59, Q39).

  taken out of its history (they stay private; the public stubs say why and when they will be reviewed again). The
  repository was then recreated under the same name so the removed commits cannot be reached by hash; issues #1-#30
  moved over with their numbers and comments. Links with the old name `sedra-merged-mining` no longer redirect.

## An archival node, and what it cannot reach

- Since 2026-11-18 Nec runs an archival Go node (sedrad v2.0.0 with `--archival`), since 2026-11-20 on its own
  virtual machine. It keeps every block from the 2026-11-18 pruning point on, so the chain from then on can be checked
  independently later.
- **It cannot reach the treasury history.** *Verified:* a new node, archival or not, only receives block bodies from
  the network's current pruning point on, and the treasury mint (DAA ~53.63M) and its spends (up to DAA ~60.53M) are
  all below it. *Inferred:* none of the public nodes we could reach still holds those blocks; the node behind the
  public explorer reports the same block count as a pruned node.
- So the treasury trace (#19) depends on the team's outgoing transaction list (Q12, due 2026-11-30) and on anyone who
  still holds an older archive (Q27, due 2026-12-14). A checker that compares the list with the chain is ready.

## What this does NOT mean

- No testnet date and no mainnet date are set. Merged mining is not active anywhere outside local test networks.
- A draft reply with promised dates is not an answer; each question stays "Asked" until the answer or data arrives.
- The advisory's protection steps reduce exposure; they do not replace a fixed sedrad release.
